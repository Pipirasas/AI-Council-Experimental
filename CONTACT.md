# Contact and discussion

For bug reports, technical proposals, first-time contributions or questions, open a **GitHub Issue** with a minimal, synthetic, reproducible example. Use **GitHub Discussions** for general design questions after the maintainer enables that feature.

There is **no project contact email published at R0**. Contributors should not seek the maintainer's personal email or post sensitive code, secrets, system logs or protected evaluation cases in public threads.

**Security-sensitive matters:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than filing detailed public reports. The public repository must enable private vulnerability reporting before publication.
