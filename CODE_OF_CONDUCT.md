# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Give specific, evidence-based feedback; do not insult, harass, dox, threaten or disclose someone's personal data. Disagree with code and reasoning rather than with people.

The maintainer may moderate contributions, lock discussions and reject abusive participation. Use GitHub's reporting tools for harassment. For security-sensitive issues, consult [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid public exploit details.
